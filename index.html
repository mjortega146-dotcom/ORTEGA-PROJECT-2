Discover KCC

Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College!

A Christ-centered learning community dedicated to developing responsible, knowledgeable, and competent individuals.

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has continued its commitment to providing quality Catholic education. Through academic excellence, faith formation, and community service, KCC helps students become responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society.

  • Promoting academic excellence and lifelong learning.
  • Strengthening faith and Christian values.
  • Developing leadership, responsibility, and service to the community.
