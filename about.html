About Kabankalan Catholic College

Catholic Education: Our Identity, Our Mission. Since 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has been committed to providing quality Catholic education that develops students in knowledge, character, competence, and faith. We aim to form responsible individuals who are ready to serve God, the community, and society.

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has been a part of the educational community of Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental for generations.

As a Catholic educational institution, KCC provides an environment where academic learning, Christian values, and community service come together. The college believes that education is not only about gaining knowledge but also about developing responsible, compassionate, and service-oriented individuals.

Guided by the teachings of Christ, KCC continues to prepare students to become productive members of society and responsible citizens of the world.

Our Philosophy

What We Believe In

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is committed to forming socially conscious, responsible, and service-oriented individuals, guided by the teachings of Christ.

We believe that education should develop the whole person through faith, knowledge, discipline, compassion, and meaningful service to others.

Our Direction

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals that leads positive change and responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

Our Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Our History

A Tradition of Catholic Education Since 1927

1927

Since its establishment in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has continued its commitment to education, faith formation, and service. Through generations, KCC has remained dedicated to helping students develop their abilities and become responsible members of society.

Today, KCC continues to uphold its mission of Catholic education and the formation of future leaders and servants of the community.
