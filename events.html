KCC Events and Activities

Campus Life

Important Events and Celebrations

KCC Foundation Day / KCC Week

Annual School Celebration

Kabankalan Catholic College celebrates its history, traditions, and achievements through its Foundation Day and KCC Week. This special celebration brings together students, teachers, employees, alumni, and the entire KCC community through different activities, performances, competitions, and meaningful programs. Founded in 1927, KCC continues its mission of providing quality Catholic education.

Hinugyaw Intramurals

Sports and Cultural Competition

Hinugyaw is an exciting school activity that promotes teamwork, sportsmanship, leadership, and friendship among students. It features different sports events, cultural competitions, presentations, and other activities that allow students to showcase their abilities and school spirit.

CMAT Pinning Ceremony

Academic Recognition and Milestone

The College of Management, Accountancy, and Technology Pinning Ceremony recognizes the dedication, perseverance, and academic journey of its students. This meaningful occasion celebrates their progress and prepares them for greater responsibilities in their chosen professional careers.

Criminology Reception Rites

Discipline, Courage, and Commitment

The Criminology Reception Rites mark an important milestone for students pursuing a career in criminology. The ceremony emphasizes discipline, courage, unity, responsibility, and commitment to serving the community and upholding justice.

Laragway Talent Night and Coronation

Talent, Beauty, and Student Leadership

Laragway highlights the talents, confidence, creativity, and leadership of KCC students. Through talent presentations and the coronation celebration, participants showcase their abilities while promoting self-confidence, teamwork, and school pride.

Udyakan Festival Participation

Culture, Arts, and Community Celebration

Kabankalan Catholic College proudly showcases the talents and creativity of its students through participation in the Udyakan Festival. Through cultural dances, performances, and artistic presentations, KCC helps promote local heritage, unity, and community pride.

Catholic Formation Activities

Faith, Prayer, and Spiritual Growth

As a Catholic educational institution, KCC values spiritual growth and faith formation. Through Eucharistic celebrations, prayer activities, recollections, retreats, and other religious activities, students are encouraged to strengthen their relationship with God and practice Christian values in their daily lives.

Recognition and Graduation Ceremony

Celebrating Achievement and Success

The Recognition and Graduation Ceremony honors students for their hard work, dedication, and achievements throughout their academic journey. It celebrates the accomplishments of graduates and recognizes students who have demonstrated excellence in academics, leadership, and other areas of school life.

Be Part of the KCC Community!

Every event is an opportunity to learn, grow, discover your talents, and create unforgettable memories. Stay connected with Kabankalan Catholic College for the latest school announcements and activities.
